## RED evidence

- Pre-fix `WeightedResourceGroupScheduler::admits` recomputed total weight,
  allocated a remainder vector and sorted every configured group on every
  request: O(G log G) work on the broker admission path.
- Public zero-byte `record_usage`/`record_release` calls accepted unknown group
  names and could grow scheduler state outside the configured registry.
- On the supported Rust 1.92 toolchain, the old scaling smoke first reported a
  false 64-byte live allocation and then aborted at contention startup because
  its global allocator depended on TLS with destructors. The same failure was
  reproduced at the pre-fix `a23bda3` source, separating proof-rail failure from
  the scheduler change.
