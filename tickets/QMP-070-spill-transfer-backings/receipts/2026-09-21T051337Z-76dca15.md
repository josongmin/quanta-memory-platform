## Qualified contract

- A successful durable restore no longer deletes the only persistent payload
  before resident publication. redb atomically moves the FIFO record into a
  recovery table and keeps the active claim until the resident side explicitly
  acknowledges it.
- Restart recovery distinguishes persisted `Prepared` and `Committed` phases.
  Both phases reuse the stable sequence and payload; a committed recovery cannot
  be aborted or reinserted under a stale identity.
- The store establishes one exact resident charge before durable commit. It
  publishes behind the mutation permit and finalizes afterward. Finalize errors
  and panics, including a panic during reconciliation, refence the resident copy
  before readers can observe it.
- Encoder panic propagation releases the store mutex before unwinding, so a
  failed transfer does not poison or permanently disable the source store.
- Deterministic redb seams cover pre-write failure plus lost acknowledgements
  after write, restore, and finalize commits. Reopen tests cover prepared claims
  and committed recovery payloads.
- redb is the only advertised production durable backing. The removed moka/scc
  indexes are not represented as canonical store backings. In-process, noop and
  redb behavior all execute in the facade/testkit semantic suites.
