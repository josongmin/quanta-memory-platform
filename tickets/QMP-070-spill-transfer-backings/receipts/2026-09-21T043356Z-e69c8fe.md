## Implemented source contract

- redb record IDs are allocated from persisted monotonic metadata and cannot be
  reused after drain/reopen. A finalized watermark rejects stale resurrection.
- durable writes borrow source bytes and classify known-not-committed versus
  recovery-required outcomes. Unknown writes fence the charged source until an
  exact persisted-record probe resolves authority.
- durable restore prepare persists one exclusive sequence claim. Decode,
  admission and known-retained failures abort it. Unknown deletes fence the
  charged resident candidate outside the readable store until persisted
  reconciliation returns retained or finalized.
- generic redb `SpillTarget` mutation methods fail closed; accounting-bearing
  controller/target marker traits are sealed to canonical implementations.
- in-process spill handles share store binding identity rather than cloning
  caller-controlled strings per entry.
- non-canonical `revocable-moka`/`revocable-scc` indexes, features, dependencies
  and matrix entries were removed. `full-backings` contains the reachable redb
  production backing only.
- the feature matrix exposed and the implementation repaired core no-default
  dead code and facade revocable-only spill-type leakage.
