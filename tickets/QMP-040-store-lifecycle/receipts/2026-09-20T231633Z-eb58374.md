## Not run / residual risk

- Not run: independent actual standalone GREEN or Semantica host QBC; do not
  interpret dry-run or repo-local doctor as either.
- Residual exceptional boundary: simultaneous client `Ord` panic and a
  panicking generic key `Drop` during `BTreeMap::entry` unwind may abort the
  process. A catch around the entry cannot intercept std's owned-key Drop;
  this is **not** covered by the tested single-callback panic contract. A full
  remedy is an index ownership/layout change with outside strong key owners,
  not a catch shim. The public key contract requires stable `Ord` and no
  panicking `Drop` during unwind; QMP-140 must surface that limitation.
- Operational tradeoff: ordinary store state-lock contention also returns
  typed Busy; QMP-100 owns contention/performance measurement.
- Byte completeness, tombstone bounds, spill transfer, and host adoption are
  separately open QMP-050/060/070/130. No production readiness claim.
