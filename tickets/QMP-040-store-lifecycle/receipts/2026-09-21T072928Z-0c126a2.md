## RED evidence

The earlier subprocess RED reproduced process abort when destructive leaves
retired multiple panicking client fields. A later static audit found that
dropping the `Err` payload returned by `catch_unwind` could itself panic during
outer unwind. The committed hostile-payload subprocess uses `panic_any` with a
panic-on-drop payload and would abort on the old discard path. The canonical
retirement helper now intentionally leaks only that exceptional caught payload;
normal values and managed charges retain their existing ownership semantics.
