## Implemented contract

- `BrokerObservation` returns ledger, policy revision/provenance, pressure,
  reclaim counters and semantic bytes from one broker-lock snapshot.
- lock poison is a public typed `MemoryError::Poisoned`; no authority path maps
  poison or corrupt observation to zero/empty state.
- public `LedgerSnapshot::checked_total_*` methods make forged/unrepresentable
  snapshots fallible rather than forcing callers through panicking totals.
- `MemoryOperation` and `MemoryOutcome` are closed label sets. Broker reserve,
  release, recharge, pressure, reclaim, reconfigure and observation paths plus
  facade close, spill, restore and tombstone saturation emit through one
  advisory sink.
- sink callbacks execute outside broker/store data locks, carry no raw identity
  label, cannot mutate semantic counters, and hostile panics are contained.

## RED evidence

Pre-fix authority exposed lock poison as generic invalid state, public forged
snapshot totals could panic without a checked alternative, and production event
adapters had no broker/store call sites. Existing negative observation fake and
new poison/event tests fail those old contracts and pass the typed implementation.
